Our story

Subs, corned beef and gyros from the heart of Maple Valley, and a shop that shows up for its neighbors.

Photo: Sam Al Jamel, owner of Hanini Subs

Big portions, fair prices, familiar faces

Hanini Subs sits at 1318 Copley Road in West Akron’s Maple Valley neighborhood. Owner Sam Al Jamel and his staff make every sub, gyro and salad to order, with portions big enough to save half for later.

The menu covers the deli classics, from cold cuts and hot subs to corned beef stacked on sub bread or rye, alongside gyros, wraps, salads and loaded fries.

For generations of Akronites, it’s been the place to grab lunch, feed the family, or bring a tray to the party.

Recognized by Akron City Council

Akron City Council issued a proclamation commending Sam Al Jamel for his dedication and service to the city. It recognizes the Copley Road shop’s support for local initiatives, youth programs and community projects, along with the food donations and outreach events Sam and his staff take part in.

Showing up for the neighborhood

  • Food donations

    Meals for neighbors and local causes when they need it most.

  • Youth programs

    Support for the programs that keep West Akron kids busy and growing.

  • Outreach events

    A welcoming counter and a regular presence at community events.
